# Blog illustration art direction

User direction: every post shares the classic Winnie-the-Pooh storybook feel. Screenshots become imaginative editorial illustrations, not painted browser windows.

## Shared visual identity

- Delicate, slightly irregular sepia ink contours with transparent watercolor washes.
- Warm ivory paper with subtle texture; generous negative space and softly fading edges.
- Restrained palette: honey ochre, moss green, dusty blue, soft clay red, sepia.
- Small original woodland characters and familiar handmade objects. Thoughtful curiosity and gentle visual humor; no glossy 3D, vector mascots, anime, neon, or photorealism.
- Horizontal 3:2 cover, ideally 1536 by 1024 pixels, with a clear focal story and crop-safe margins. Readable at thumbnail size.
- No rendered titles or dense labels. Hugo provides accessible text outside the artwork.

## Screenshot translation

1. Identify the technical story supported by the screenshot and reviewed article.
2. Crop or exclude browser chrome, bookmarks, customer names, domains, account identifiers, and other private data before sending reference images.
3. Translate the story into a coherent woodland metaphor. Repetition can become paper trails; retained artifacts can become folders; a diagnostic clue can become a magnifying glass.
4. Do not invent measured graphs, counts, causes, successful remediation, or observed product behavior. Artwork is interpretation, never evidence.
5. Use the first published cover as a visual reference for later illustrations, along with the new screenshot. Preserve palette, pen weight, texture, scale, and composition rhythm while changing the scene for the post's subject.
6. Inspect the actual output for privacy, visual consistency, unwanted text, and misleading factual claims. Publish only a finished illustration, with GhostWriter-generated alt text and caption describing it as illustrative.

## Reusable prompt core

Create a vintage British woodland storybook editorial illustration: loose delicate sepia pen-and-ink lines and soft transparent watercolor washes on warm ivory textured paper. Muted honey ochre, moss green, dusty blue, clay red. Intimate scale, thoughtful original woodland characters, gentle observational humor, generous negative space, softly fading edges. One clear scene conveying the article's diagnostic story. No browser UI, private identifiers, labels, invented measurements, photorealism, 3D, neon, anime, or modern vector art. Horizontal 3:2, crop-safe margins, readable at thumbnail size. Match the reference cover's linework and palette.

## First cover

The evtSpike cover transforms the supplied Event Spikes screenshot's six timeline rows and crimson dots into a paper chart on a woodland noticeboard. A thoughtful bear examines a larger dot with a magnifying glass, a field mouse takes notes, and a modest stack of folders suggests accumulated artifacts. It conveys detection and accumulation, not a confirmed vendor defect or completed cleanup.

Reference asset for future generations: `content/posts/a-mystery-uncovered-with-evtspike/evtspike-storybook.webp`. The supplied screenshot is preserved separately as `evtspike-reference.webp`; its later dates and counts are not the September 26 RCA measurements.
